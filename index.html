Cast by holding your action key and releasing at the power you want. Sink to the depth the fish are holding at, then tap your action key to work the lure — that jigging action is what draws a strike.
When you see !, tap again fast to set the hook. In the fight, hold to reel: it gains line but loads the rod. Let go and the drag gives it back. Push past the white mark and the line starts fraying — that damage never heals, so muscle a fish for long enough and it will part. Pump and wind instead.
Co-op: when your partner is hooked up and your line is in the boat, hold your action key to work the net — it bleeds off their tension and pays you both. Land two fish at once for a double header bonus.
Progress: rods carry heavier fish, reels hold more line and unlock deeper spots, lures decide which species even look at you. Money is shared, so plan the upgrades together. Every species fights differently: pike run, catfish sulk, bass jump. Your panel tells you which you have on.
